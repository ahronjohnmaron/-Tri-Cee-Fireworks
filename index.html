Celebrate responsibly

Great nights start with safe choices.

Fireworks are regulated products. Buy from an authorized seller, verify the product label, and follow every manufacturer and local-government instruction.

Use outdoors onlyChoose a wide, clear area away from people, homes, vehicles, dry grass and overhead lines.
Never hold a cakePlace it upright on level ground, stabilize it as directed, light once, and move away immediately.
Keep water readyHave a hose or bucket nearby. Never relight a dud; wait and follow disposal guidance.
Adults onlyKeep children and pets at a safe distance. Observe local rules, permits and quiet hours.
